ABOUT THE

ERHTCI PORTAL

♛

An institutional feedback portal for reporting, monitoring, and service improvement.

Purpose

The ERHTCI Feedback Portal collects feedback about training facilities, instruction, and operational procedures so the institution can review concerns and improve services.

Who Can Use It?

  • Students
  • Staff
  • Instructors

How Feedback Is Processed

  1. Submit a concern or feedback.
  2. The administrator reviews the report and updates its status.
  3. The report is marked Pending or Under Review.
  4. After action, the report is marked Resolved.
  5. The user can monitor the status through the profile.

Submission Rules & Privacy

What should I submit?

Provide clear, factual, and respectful feedback related to the institution, training, facilities, or operations.

Can I submit anonymously?

Yes. Anonymous submissions are linked to your account for private status tracking, but your name is hidden from public feedback cards. Everyone signed in can see feedback statuses and administrator responses. The administrator can see the submitter identity.

How is data handled?

Feedback and accounts are stored in the configured database. Anonymous feedback hides the submitter name from public views, not from the administrator. Use this system only after reviewing the institution's privacy policy, access rules, and deployment security.
